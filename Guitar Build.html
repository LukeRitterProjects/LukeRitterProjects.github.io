Travel Guitar (2026)

This project was my first attempt at building any sort of acoustic instrument. I purposefully chose to design this guitar without looking at other designs, aside from a Crimson Guitars project that I remember seeing a few years ago. That's why the neck joint is odd and certain steps in the process were poorly done. The final product sounds good- but is far from perfect. Lots of lessons to apply next time.
