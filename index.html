Welcome! Here you'll find my Bio, CV, and work samples. On other pages, you'll find personal projects, interests, and creative endeavors.

I'm a forester, outdoor educator, and social scientist working in Flagstaff, Arizona. I received my Master's in Forestry in 2025 from Northern Arizona University, then published my research on human-technology interaction in natural resources. I taught throughout graduate school and now am involved in various teaching, stewardship, and educational programs.
