Who I Am

I'm a forester, outdoor educator, and social scientist working in Flagstaff, Arizona. I grew up exploring the high deserts and spring-fed rivers of central AZ before pursuing a career in natural resources. I'm passionate about acquiring new skills to frame my understanding of the world and inform my passion for science communication. In my free time, I enjoy luthiery, writing music, mountain biking, and film photography.
